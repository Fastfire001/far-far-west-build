Far Far West Build Planner

A free build planner and build maker for Far Far West, the co-op western-fantasy shooter by Evil Raptor. Plan your build: main weapon, sidearm and its element (Acid, Pyro, Elec or Frost), utility, three spells, stat upgrades and jokers for the hero and each weapon. All data is extracted from the game files, up to date with version {gameVersion}, with the game's texts in its 15 languages.

What the planner does

  • Checks the build: 26 upgrade points and 16 joker slots per carrier, max copies of each joker, jokers that fit the weapon, no duplicate spell.
  • Shows the minimum level and the number of prestiges the hero, the main weapon and the sidearm need, Unique jokers included.
  • Shows the cooldown of each spell.
  • Saves your builds in the browser and shares them with a link, no account needed.
